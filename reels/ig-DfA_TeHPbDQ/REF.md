- Caption on the post: "Math is hard. Timezone math is even harder. Leo manages creators across the world, and coordinating one call across 5 timezones took longer than the call itself. So Ying made an app for Leo, to fix that. Get yourself a friend like Ying 🫶"

## Format

This is a creator-story ad told by the builder. Ying narrates in voiceover and appears on camera. It's a fast collage of:

- handheld live action shot on a wide or fisheye lens (heavy vignette, warm green-yellow grade, film grain)
- webcam and Zoom/Meet screen recordings
- hand-made paper props: marker lettering on coloured card, a "report card" poster, cut-out stickers slapped on by a hand
- stop-motion kinetic type on white paper, with marker-scribble boxes that redraw every frame (line boil)
- a real wall map with a red marker
- screen recordings of the Lovable prompt and the app it built ("LinkUp!")

There are about 68 cuts in 76 s, so a cut every ~1.1 s.

**Two caption styles:**
1. **Word pop:** white heavy grotesk (Inter/Helvetica Bold look), one word appearing per spoken word and scattered around the subject, sometimes over 2–3 lines.
2. **Small subtitle:** white, bottom centre, small bold sans, shown over longer lines.

Some key words are physical stickers instead of captions: LEO, FAIL, TIME, LOCATION, WORKING ON, PRIORITIES, ANYTIME, MAYBE?, LEAVE ME ALONE!, A BETTER WAY, PASS!.

**Music and sound:** an upbeat indie bed under the VO, plus paper-slap and marker foley.

## What carries the ad

- **A real-friend story.** The builder made the app for a friend whose job depends on it. The stakes are shown with the report card going from FAIL to PASS (bookends at shots 7 and 49–50).
- **Handmade texture.** Every key word is a physical paper sign or sticker, so the props carry the edit. Nothing looks like stock motion graphics.
- **The product appears late (at 44 s) and briefly.** It's a 3 s prompt, then the result app's features, each one landed with a sticker word.
- **Lo-fi look.** Wide or fisheye phone footage, webcam recordings, direct-flash photos, and a warm green-cast grade with grain and vignette.
- **CTA:** comment "LINK UP" for the link, then the "a Lovable story" end card.
