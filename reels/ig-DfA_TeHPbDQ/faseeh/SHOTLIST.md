# Shoot list: "This is Faseeh" (to film tonight)

Shoot on a phone. Settings and style:
- **Phone settings:** 4K or 1080p, **24 fps**, horizontal. The edit is 4:3, so keep the action in the middle and leave room on both sides.
- **Look of the reference (LinkUp):**
  - handheld, close, slightly low angles
  - 0.5× ultra-wide for the comedic shots
  - real messy desks and lamps
  - everyone moving: no locked tripod shots
- **Takes:** film every shot 2–3 times, each about 3–4 seconds long (the edit uses 0.5–1.5 s of each).
